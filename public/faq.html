Plastic Surgery Questions

Questions about surgical procedures and recovery

Good candidates for plastic surgery typically share these characteristics:

Ideal Candidates:
  • In good general health without uncontrolled medical conditions
  • Non-smokers or willing to quit 4-6 weeks before and after surgery
  • Have realistic expectations about outcomes
  • Seeking improvement, not perfection
  • Emotionally stable and well-informed
May Not Be Candidates:
  • Uncontrolled diabetes, heart disease, or bleeding disorders
  • Active smokers unwilling to quit
  • Unrealistic expectations or body dysmorphia
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Significant weight fluctuations

Important: Only a comprehensive consultation with our board-certified plastic surgeons can determine your candidacy.

Note: Recovery times vary significantly by procedure, individual healing, and following post-operative instructions carefully.

Scars are a natural part of the healing process after surgery, but their appearance can be significantly minimized:

Surgical Techniques:
  • Placement in natural creases or hidden areas
  • Layered closure techniques for better healing
  • Minimal tension on incision lines
Scar Treatments:
  • Silicon sheets or gels during healing
  • Laser treatments for scar revision
  • Microneedling with PRP to improve texture
  • Steroid injections for raised scars

Scar Evolution: Scars typically mature over 12-18 months, becoming lighter, flatter, and less noticeable with proper care.

Tips for Optimal Scar Healing:
  • Keep incisions clean and moisturized
  • Protect from sun exposure for at least 1 year
  • Massage scars as recommended by your surgeon
  • Avoid smoking and maintain good nutrition

Factors Affecting Duration: Individual metabolism, treatment area, product used, amount injected, and lifestyle factors can all affect how long results last.

Most cosmetic injectables have minimal downtime, but here's what to expect:

Immediate After Effects:
  • Minor redness, swelling, or bruising at injection sites
  • Small bumps or lumps that resolve within hours to days
  • Tenderness in treated areas

These typically resolve within 24-72 hours.

Activity Restrictions:
  • 24 hours: Avoid strenuous exercise, alcohol, and blood thinners
  • 48 hours: Avoid facial massage or pressure on treated areas
  • 1 week: Avoid extreme heat (saunas, hot yoga) and sun exposure
Quick Recovery Tips:
  • Apply cold compresses to reduce swelling
  • Sleep with head elevated for first night
  • Use arnica cream or supplements to reduce bruising
  • Schedule treatments when you don't have important events

Dermatology Questions

Questions about skin health, conditions, and treatments

Skin cancer screening frequency depends on your risk factors:

High Risk (Annual Screening):
  • Personal history of skin cancer
  • Family history of melanoma
  • Fair skin, light eyes, red or blonde hair
  • History of severe sunburns
  • Many moles or atypical moles
  • Immunosuppressed individuals
Average Risk (Every 1-2 Years):
  • No personal or family history of skin cancer
  • Moderate sun exposure history
  • Few moles, all normal appearing
  • Good sun protection habits
Monthly Self-Exams:

Regardless of screening frequency, we recommend monthly self-examinations using the ABCDE rule:

Asymmetry
Border irregularity
Color variation
Diameter >6mm
Evolving/changing
