Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about cosmetic procedures

Are cosmetic procedures painful? +

Most cosmetic procedures involve minimal discomfort. For injectables like Botox and fillers, we use ultra-fine needles and may apply topical numbing cream to ensure your comfort. Laser treatments and chemical peels may cause mild sensations during the procedure, but these are typically well-tolerated. We prioritize your comfort throughout every treatment.

How long do results from cosmetic procedures last? +

Results vary depending on the treatment and individual factors. Botox typically lasts 3-4 months, while dermal fillers can last 6-18 months. Chemical peels provide results that can last several months to a year, while laser treatments may offer results lasting 1-5 years. Kybella provides permanent reduction of fat cells in the treated area. Maintenance treatments are usually recommended to sustain optimal results.

Is there downtime after cosmetic procedures? +

Most non-surgical procedures have minimal to no downtime. Injectables like Botox and fillers may cause minor redness or swelling that resolves within a few hours to a day. Chemical peels and laser treatments may require 3-7 days of social downtime depending on the intensity. We provide detailed aftercare instructions to ensure optimal healing and results.

How do I know which treatment is right for me? +

During your consultation, our specialists will assess your skin type, concerns, and goals to recommend the most appropriate treatments. We consider factors like your age, skin condition, desired results, and lifestyle. Often, a combination of treatments provides the best outcome. We prioritize natural-looking results that enhance your unique features.

Are cosmetic procedures safe? +

When performed by qualified medical professionals in a clinical setting, cosmetic procedures are very safe. At Grace Glow, all treatments are administered by board-certified specialists using FDA-approved products and medical-grade equipment. We conduct thorough consultations to ensure you're a good candidate for the procedure and discuss any potential risks or side effects.
