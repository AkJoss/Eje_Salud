Plastic Surgery FAQs

Common questions about surgical procedures

Ideal candidates are in good general health, have realistic expectations, don't smoke, and are seeking improvement (not perfection). During your consultation, Dr. Johnson will evaluate your medical history and aesthetic goals to determine suitability.

Recovery varies by procedure. Most patients return to light activities within 1-2 weeks, with full recovery taking several weeks to months. We provide detailed recovery guidelines and support throughout your healing process.

While plastic surgery produces long-lasting results, your body continues to age naturally. Procedures like facelifts typically last 7-10+ years, while breast implants may need replacement after 10-15 years. We focus on natural aging alongside your surgical results.

Our facility is fully accredited, with board-certified anesthesiologists, modern monitoring equipment, and strict sterilization protocols. Dr. Johnson maintains hospital privileges and follows evidence-based surgical techniques for maximum safety.
