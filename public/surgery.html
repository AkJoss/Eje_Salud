Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about non-surgical treatments

Most patients experience minimal discomfort. We use ultra-fine needles and can apply topical numbing cream before treatment. Many fillers also contain lidocaine, a local anesthetic, to enhance comfort during the procedure.

Duration varies by treatment: Botox® typically lasts 3-4 months, dermal fillers 6-18 months, Kybella® provides permanent fat reduction, and laser treatments can last 1-3 years with proper maintenance. We'll provide specific timelines during your consultation.

Botox results appear in 3-7 days, with full effect at 2 weeks. Fillers show immediate results, though initial swelling may slightly obscure the final outcome. Laser treatments and chemical peels show progressive improvement over several weeks as new collagen forms.

Common temporary side effects include redness, swelling, bruising, or tenderness at the treatment site. These typically resolve within a few hours to a few days. Serious complications are rare when treatments are performed by experienced providers using proper techniques.
