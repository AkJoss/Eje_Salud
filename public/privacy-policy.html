Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 15, 2026

Protecting your privacy is our priority
HIPAA Notice

As a healthcare provider, Grace Glow complies with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and protects your Protected Health Information (PHI) according to applicable laws.

This Privacy Policy describes how Grace Glow Cosmetic Medicine ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website www.graceglow.com and when you use our services. By using our website or services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information You Provide

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Schedule an appointment or consultation
  • Complete contact forms on our website
  • Sign up for our newsletter or promotions
  • Participate in surveys or provide feedback
  • Communicate with us via email, phone, or in person

This information may include:

  • Contact information (name, email, phone, address)
  • Demographic information (age, gender)
  • Medical history and health information
  • Payment information
  • Insurance information
1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information, including:

  • IP address and device information
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website or search terms
  • Clickstream data
1.3 Protected Health Information (PHI)

As a healthcare provider, we collect and maintain Protected Health Information as defined by HIPAA, including:

  • Medical records and treatment history
  • Laboratory and test results
  • Treatment plans and progress notes
  • Billing and insurance information

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Primary Uses

We use your information for the following purposes:

Medical Treatment

To provide medical services, treatment, and care

Appointments

To schedule, confirm, and manage appointments

Billing & Payment

To process payments and handle billing

Healthcare Operations

To improve quality of care and practice operations

2.2 Communication

We may use your contact information to:

  • Send appointment reminders and confirmations
  • Provide treatment follow-up information
  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Send practice updates and newsletters (with consent)
  • Inform you about new services or promotions
2.3 Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information based on:

  • Consent: When you have given clear consent
  • Contract: To fulfill our obligations to you
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with laws and regulations
  • Legitimate Interests: For our legitimate business interests
  • Vital Interests: To protect your vital interests

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

3.1 When We Share Information

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

Healthcare Providers

With other healthcare providers involved in your treatment

Business Associates

With third parties who provide services to our practice

Legal Requirements

When required by law, court order, or government request

Emergency Situations

In emergency situations to protect your health

3.2 Third-Party Service Providers

We work with third-party service providers who help us operate our practice, including:

  • Electronic medical records systems
  • Billing and payment processors
  • Website hosting and analytics providers
  • Marketing and communication services
  • Appointment scheduling software

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

3.3 What We Do NOT Do
We do NOT sell your personal information
We do NOT share your information for marketing purposes without consent
We do NOT disclose Protected Health Information except as permitted by HIPAA

4. Data Security

Our Commitment to Security

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your personal and health information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

4.1 Security Measures

We employ the following security measures:

  • Encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest
  • Secure servers with firewalls and intrusion detection
  • Regular security assessments and audits
  • Employee training on privacy and security
  • Access controls and authentication protocols
  • Physical security measures at our facilities
4.2 HIPAA Compliance

As a covered entity under HIPAA, we:

  • Implement administrative, physical, and technical safeguards
  • Conduct regular risk assessments
  • Maintain Business Associate Agreements with third parties
  • Provide HIPAA training to all employees
  • Maintain Notice of Privacy Practices
4.3 Incident Response

In the event of a data breach, we will:

  1. Immediately investigate and contain the breach
  2. Notify affected individuals as required by law
  3. Report to appropriate regulatory authorities
  4. Take steps to prevent future breaches

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Right to Access

Request access to your personal information we hold

Right to Correction

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information

Right to Deletion

Request deletion of your personal information in certain circumstances

Right to Restrict Processing

Request restriction of processing your information

Right to Data Portability

Request transfer of your data to another provider

Right to Object

Object to processing of your personal information

5.2 HIPAA Rights

Under HIPAA, you have specific rights regarding your Protected Health Information:

  • Right to receive our Notice of Privacy Practices
  • Right to access and obtain copies of your medical records
  • Right to request amendments to your medical records
  • Right to an accounting of disclosures
  • Right to request restrictions on certain uses and disclosures
  • Right to request confidential communications
  • Right to file a complaint about privacy practices
5.3 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in Section 10. We may require verification of your identity before processing your request.

Note: Some rights may be limited by applicable laws, such as HIPAA, which may prevent us from deleting certain medical records.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

6.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide a better user experience and understand how visitors use our site.

6.2 Types of Cookies We Use
Essential Cookies

Required for the website to function properly

Required
Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors use our website

Optional
Preference Cookies

Remember your preferences and settings

Optional
Marketing Cookies

Used for targeted advertising and marketing

Optional
6.3 Cookie Management

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete cookies individually or all at once
  • Block cookies from specific sites or all sites
  • Set preferences for different types of cookies

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

6.4 Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers have a "Do Not Track" feature that lets you tell websites that you do not want to have your online activities tracked. We currently do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals.

7. Children's Privacy

Children Under 13: Our website is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
7.1 Minors and Medical Treatment

For medical treatment of minors, we:

  • Require parental or guardian consent for treatment
  • Collect information from parents or guardians
  • Provide treatment information to parents or guardians
  • Comply with state laws regarding minor consent
7.2 Parental Rights

Parents or guardians of minors have the right to:

  • Access their child's medical records
  • Request corrections to their child's records
  • Make treatment decisions for their child
  • Request restrictions on information sharing

If you believe we may have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. Data Retention

8.1 Retention Periods

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Medical Records
Minimum 7 years from last treatment (varies by state)
Billing Records
7 years from date of service
Website Analytics
26 months
Marketing Communications
Until consent is withdrawn
8.2 Data Disposal

When information is no longer needed, we securely dispose of it using methods such as:

  • Secure shredding of paper records
  • Digital wiping of electronic media
  • Professional destruction services
  • Secure deletion from databases

9. International Data Transfers

9.1 Data Location

Grace Glow is based in the United States, and we process and store information in the United States. If you are located outside the United States, please be aware that information we collect will be transferred to and processed in the United States.

9.2 Cross-Border Transfers

We may transfer your information to service providers or partners in other countries. When we do so, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as:

  • Standard contractual clauses
  • Privacy Shield certification (where applicable)
  • Adequacy decisions
  • Binding corporate rules
9.3 Your Consent

By using our website or services, you consent to the transfer, processing, and storage of your information in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate.
