Medical Spa FAQs

Common questions about our spa treatments

Your first visit begins with a warm welcome and consultation. We'll discuss your skin concerns, goals, and medical history. You'll receive a tour of our facilities, change into a plush robe, and enjoy herbal tea in our relaxation lounge. Your aesthetician will then guide you through your personalized treatment plan. Most first visits last 1.5-2 hours including consultation and treatment.

Most of our spa treatments have minimal to no downtime. HydraFacial and LED therapy have zero downtime. Microneedling may cause 24-48 hours of redness. We provide specific aftercare instructions for each treatment. You can typically return to normal activities immediately after most treatments, though we recommend avoiding strenuous exercise and sun exposure for 24 hours.
